What does not change

The system can become more capable without becoming unaccountable.

The identity stays: intelligence that evolves. The method stays: proof before claims. The boundary stays: human authority before high-risk motion.

Read the doctrine→ Map the architecture
Authority
Humans approve filesystem writes, money movement, public posting, and high-risk automation.
Proof
Every serious claim points to an artifact, test, transmission, status label, or explicit unknown.
Simulation
Web3 intelligence starts read-only, unsigned, no-broadcast, and reviewable.
Cells
Specialists remain replaceable so the organism can evolve without becoming locked to one tool or provider.
Identity
The core stays intelligence that evolves: memory, boundaries, proof, and clearer action over time.
