A FEW QUESTIONS

About the organism.

What is an AI organism?+

A software system with a cortex that reasons, memory that persists, cells that work, and a proof loop. Its development happens within boundaries defined by people.

Where can I inspect proof?+

Records and architecture notes are in the proof ledger and the transmission archive.

Can it move money or publish on its own?+

No. Financial actions and outward publications require human approval. These illustrations represent concepts, rather than evidence of live execution.

What happens when it fails?+

The failure should be inspectable. Incidents and lessons are recorded so changes can be reviewed and tested.
