02

Cheap by default, not by discipline

Observed, then outlook

Observed · On 27 August 2026 the operator ruled that every cloud-model call this organism makes is routed to the cheapest model that still meets that task's required intelligence floor, with local models staying the default so a cloud call is a deliberate choice, under absolute dollar caps that only a separate ruling — never an agent — may raise.

Outlook · Agentic systems that have to pay their own way may keep defaulting to the cheapest adequate model rather than the most capable one for everything — because that discipline only holds if it is chosen on purpose, not taken as an afterthought.

03

A record remembers; a prompt doesn't

Observed, then outlook

Observed · On 8 November 2026 a single shared file, read by every agent before it starts work, replaced a prior pattern of re-telling each agent separately what was already in progress; within the same day more than one kind of agent claimed and finished jobs from that one file without collision.

Outlook · Shared, journaled records may end up doing more of the coordination work between agents than any single prompt, simply because a prompt does not survive past its own session and a shared record does.

04

Authority stayed with a wallet, not a model

Observed, then outlook

Observed · Across the record from 23 August through 9 November 2026, the organism's financial design keeps the keys to its reserve off this machine at every stage, with no stage of spending authority granted yet; any future grant is designed to require a human-signed ruling naming an absolute cap and an expiry date, never an open-ended or percentage-based permission.

Outlook · Keeping authority over irreversible acts — money above all — with a human-held key seems likely to persist for a long time, not because agents cannot be trusted with judgment, but because judgment and custody are different problems, and only one of them is reversible to get wrong.

05

Counting only what has a receipt

Observed, then outlook

Observed · A planning document dated 8 November 2026 counts organism-wide progress only from verifiable receipts, and by its own count that week showed zero executed tasks with a verified receipt and zero of the prior fourteen nightly checks clean.

Observed · On 29 August 2026 the usable ceiling for the organism's local model was redefined from a fixed, assumed safety margin to a live-checked one — reading the hardware's actual temperature and memory load before every load — rather than a number picked once and trusted indefinitely.

Outlook · Counting only receipts, not claims, is uncomfortable because it makes slow, real progress look identical to no progress at all on a chart — but systems that adopt it anyway may end up more trustworthy for it, because they may stop grading their own homework.
