TRANSMISSION 052

Constraints That
Make Autonomy Possible

Two wallets, never one by policy alone: a reserve whose keys never touch this machine, and a small working float that is the only wallet any spending grant could ever touch. Compromise the float and you lose only the float; the reserve is unreachable by construction.

November 2026·Field note·Philosophy·7 min read
01

Two wallets, never one

The reserve stays off the machine; the float is all a grant could ever touch

The design keeps two wallets structurally separate, never just by policy: a reserve that holds the real treasury, whose keys never touch this machine at any stage, ever — an agent may only read its balance and generate a receiving address, nothing else — and a small working float, topped up deliberately, which is the only wallet any later spending grant could ever touch.

A full compromise of the working float costs only the float. The reserve is unreachable by construction.

02

A ladder, never a leap

No stage promotes itself; each one has to be earned

Any future spending authority is earned one stage at a time, and no stage promotes itself: watch-only today, then a stage where an agent may prepare a transaction but a human signs every single one, then a stage where a human approves each specific transaction before it broadcasts, and only after all of that, a narrowly-typed bounded stage for one pre-declared, repeatable action — never a general spending power.

Every promotion requires both a vote and a separate signed ruling naming the stage, its limits and an end date. Today none of that is granted: watch-only is still the only stage in force.

03

A cap in dollars, never a share

Every cap is absolute, fixed at the time of the ruling

Every cap is a fixed, absolute figure decided at the moment of the ruling — never a percentage of holdings. A percentage cap grows quietly as the treasury grows and turns an apparently small permission into an unbounded one.

A full grant has to state five numbers at once: a per-transaction ceiling, a per-day ceiling, a lifetime ceiling, a fixed list of destinations decided in advance, and an expiry date, after which the grant reverts to watch-only by default. Silence renews nothing.

Two caps, two outcomes

A cap as a percentage of holdings

Looks small at signing. Grows with the treasury whether anyone resizes it or not.

    A cap as a fixed dollar figure set at the time of the ruling

    Stays fixed no matter how the treasury moves. Reviewable, bounded, reverting at expiry.

      The first quietly grows with the treasury. The second does not.

      04

      What has to be proven before any of it opens

      Four rungs, each unlocked by its own ceremony, none unlocking on its own

      No stage promotes itself. Every promotion requires both a vote and a separate signed ruling naming the stage, its limits and an end date — and each rung unlocks only through its own ceremony, never on its own.

      Today none of that has been granted. Watch-only is still the only stage in force.

      01

      Stage 0 · Watch only

      In force today · zero execution authority of any kind. Nothing an agent prepares can broadcast on its own.

      02

      Stage 1 · Propose, human signs

      Not granted · an agent may prepare a transaction, but a human signs every single one. Unlocked by a vote plus a signed ruling naming the stage, its limits and an end date.

      03

      Stage 2 · Approve per transaction

      Not granted · a human approves each specific transaction before it broadcasts.

      04

      Stage 3 · Bounded autonomy

      Not granted · one pre-declared, repeatable action, narrowly typed — never a general spending power.

      05

      When in doubt, the stricter reading wins

      The design's own tie-breaker, escalated to a human

      If any part of the rule is ever unclear or contested, the answer is always the stricter reading, escalated to a human rather than resolved by the system.

      "The cost of an agent doing nothing is delay. The cost of an agent doing the wrong thing with money is not recoverable."

      2 enforcements · watch-only today · 0 spending stages granted

      No spending stage is live

      Watch-only today. Nothing on this page describes a granted spending power, and none will be until a ruling grants one.

      Constraints are what make autonomy possible.

      Draw the boundary before the code.

      If you are moving an autonomous system onto a chain and the real question is what must never move with it, we should talk.
