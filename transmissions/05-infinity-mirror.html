TRANSMISSION 005

The Infinity Mirror:
Personal Intelligence

An April 2026 note described the Infinity Mirror as a mobile coach with identity profiles, growth trees and flawless recall. Checked against the record, a coaching cell with a small measurement loop exists, an app survives only as a roadmap and design documents, most promised features do not, and the mirror's own charter forbids flattery and diagnosis.

November 2026·Revised note·Organisms·9 min read
Dated. This note describes the code and documents as they stand on 10 November 2026. The app's own status is dated by its last edit, 24 February 2026. The note it replaces was published in April 2026.

Revised 10 November 2026

Rewritten to the Transmission standard. Nine claims were checked: four withdrawn, two softened, two metaphors and one design. None is fully supported as written. The page now says what exists and gives an honest status for each promise, including the ones that were never built.

Correction, 10 November 2026

The April note said the mirror used identity profiles and growth trees, collected a baseline of values and directives, rewrote the mobile app's own configuration, and remembered every session flawlessly through retrieval and SQLite state. The record supports none of this. The app's roadmap, last edited 24 February 2026, lists saving journal entries as not done, and no later record shows otherwise. Those sentences are withdrawn, and "brutally honest coach" is softened to what the charter says.

01

Nine claims from April, checked

A ledger of what the old note said

The April note introduced the Infinity Mirror as the most intimate of the organisms: a self-reflecting mobile intelligence meant to close the gap between who a person is and who they intend to be. It rested on a concept it called Identity Drift, the way a person, or an agent, loses track of its goals for lack of objective tracking. Against that, the mirror would hold an Identity Profile and a Growth Tree, calibrate continuously against a person's stated directives, and correct course with unsentimental honesty.

It also described how: the app would persist progress by rewriting its own configuration, give each user a containerised assistant, and remember every session flawlessly through retrieval and direct state transitions, acting as an external lobe of the hippocampus.

This is a product description, and a product is the easiest thing to check, because a product leaves screens, a backend and a roadmap. We split the note into nine claims. None survives as written. Filter the ledger and open each card.

Four withdrawn, two softened, two metaphors, one design. There is no supported row, and that is the finding.

02

What exists under the name

Four pieces, and four things that share a name

Four pieces are on record. A coaching cell in the Brain wraps the emotional-analysis logic. A mobile app is described by a roadmap and design documents in its own folder of the Brain repository; the 8 November audit found no app source on this machine. A channel for the mirror sits in the Brain's gateway. And a charter fixes what the mirror may never do. Open each one below to see what the record shows.

The charter itself carries a useful warning: four different things share the mirror's name. Before reading further, it helps to separate them.

03

What a mirror may never do

Charter law, not observed behaviour

The charter is the most solid thing on the record, and it contradicts the note's tone. Where the note promised brutal honesty, the charter asks for accuracy with kindness: a mirror that reflects a person with enough accuracy to see the pattern, and with enough kindness to bear to look. It says a mirror is useless if it flatters, and it says it is not a therapist.

Choose a situation below. The left column is a generic assistant that does whatever it is asked; the right is what the charter requires. This is written law, not observed behaviour. No running app is shown here, and the model makes no claim about any real product's conduct. One piece is implemented beyond text: the Brain's operator surfaces and gateway summaries are built to carry counts and states instead of content.

      The record, in dates

      How the claim and the product diverged

      The dates matter more than usual here, because the product's own status is old. Its last documented state is nearly nine months before this note.

      01

      20 January and 3 July 2026 · The cell

      The emotions cell, whose header is dated 20 January, wraps the coaching logic from the mirror app. On 3 July it gained scoring, a trend and a reflection question, with 18 test functions.

      02

      24 February 2026 · The roadmap's last edit

      The app's roadmap reads 60 percent complete and lists screens, a backend, an NLP engine and motion design as done. Saving a journal entry and showing patterns are unchecked. The 8 November audit found only design documents on this machine, so these statuses are the roadmap's own and could not be checked against source.

      03

      April 2026 · The note

      Describes identity profiles, growth trees and flawless recall as if they existed.

      04

      8 November 2026 · The audit

      The consolidation audit classes the mirror dormant and doc-only: a scripted static demo, design documents and PDFs, and no app source on the machine. It proposes retiring the separate code, keeping the concept, and building any revival on the main memory. That decision waits on the operator.

      Rewritten against the record. No claim about users, outcomes or recall survives, because no users or outcomes are recorded.

      Nothing between February and November changes the app's status in the record. That is not evidence the work stopped. It is evidence that nothing was written down.

      06

      What this note does not claim

      Boundary
      • It does not claim the app has users, releases or outcomes. None are recorded.
      • It does not claim any therapeutic or clinical effect. The charter forbids the claim.
      • It does not claim the July 2026 scoring and reflection code has met a real person's journal. Its tests use synthetic text.
      • It does not claim identity profiles, growth trees, a baseline intake or per-user containers exist.
      • It does not claim recall of any kind. The save step is listed as not done.
      • It does not claim the app is still being built. The 8 November audit lists the mirror dormant and doc-only.
      • It does not claim the charter's rules have been audited in use. They are written law, and one is implemented in the Brain's surfaces.

      What survives is an intent and a boundary: a mirror that reflects accurately, never flatters, never diagnoses and never leaks what it is shown. The intent is worth keeping. The product has yet to earn the word.
