TRANSMISSION 039

The Entrance
Came Back

Built on 5 and 6 November from one script and a reference reel's tempo, the twenty-five second finish passed twenty of twenty checks and was called final. Then a note about the opening drone was read as "replace the opening," and the approved walk-in entrance was removed without asking: "Why would we change the original entrance?"

November 2026·Field note·Production·7 min read
01

Twenty of twenty, and still wrong

The finish passed every check. Then the operator looked beside the reference.

Five and six November. One script, one reference reel's tempo, a twenty-five second property reel with a virtual host. The six November finish passed twenty of twenty measured checks and was called final.

Then the review: transitions too rough, the drone opening missing, fonts and captions "can be much better", a flat grade, music not following the sound design. The QA had measured its own spec, not the reference; nothing had put the export beside the reference. Twenty of twenty, and still wrong.

The lesson

Approved elements had no lock. Every note now becomes a change list plus a LOCKED list, and an ambiguous note is asked back before work.

02

Why change the original entrance?

An approved element removed without asking.

A note about "the initial impacting drone footage" was read as "replace the opening," and the approved walk-in entrance was removed without asking. The operator: "Why would we change the original entrance?"

Corrections: open as the original opens; a whoosh at the end of the first line onto a new drone push; no song — an agent that adds songs comes later. The lesson: approved elements had no lock. Every note now becomes a change list plus a LOCKED list, and an ambiguous note is asked back before work.

03

Speed-matched through the cut

Rendered the night of 6 November · 25.333 s · 760 frames at 30 fps

Every moving transition speed-matched through the cut: the outgoing accelerates, the incoming starts at that speed and settles, blur follows speed, no flash, luma change 0-6.5 %, zooms to 1.45x out and from 0.73x in.

Every clip was regraded toward the reference and baked per clip before compositing — one sky, one warm white. Captions in an open-licence typeface, per-letter animators over 5 frames, 17 groups inside 0.14-0.65 of frame height, contrast at least 4.37:1. One new walk up the real drive, its video call carrying one card only, ruled "use it, note it." Sound: voice and effects only, -14.0 LUFS, -2.0 dBTP, gaps left silent for the music agent.

Four layers rebuilt · sound left open for the music agent
04

The day, as it ran

Five November to seven November, in order

Five November: a 15-second cut. Six November: the 25-second finish in three rounds, 20 of 20. Six November evening: the operator's notes and rulings. That night: the rebuild. Seven November: the written review, and nine production seats hired (Transmission 40).

05

What the QA could measure

Format, loudness, transitions, flash, grade

The rebuild's QA measured format; loudness -14 LUFS ±1, true peak at most -1.5 dBTP; smooth transitions (no one-frame motion spike above 1.8x its neighbours at a cut; motion before within 2x of after); no flash (mean luma within 12 %); no black or frozen frames; per-shot grade statistics.

Results: speed matched on 7 of 8 moving cuts; sound 9 of 9 (7 pass, 2 music checks not applicable); voice placed within 0.04 ms; every effect peaks 10 ms into its cut. Not checked by ear: nobody had listened on the machine.

06

Not posted, on purpose

Spend finished; four walking shots left unmade

Spend: 45.2 credits (start frames 14; drone 7 + 7, one rejected for a quadcopter in frame; the walk 7; music and effects 10.2); balance 5.75 after. About 370 credits over three rounds in all. The operator: "no more topping up. finish it with what you have."

Still waiting: a listen by ear; the rights check for the listing photos and an AI host presenting a listing; the accent check; the music seat; posting — the operator's hand. Not posted. Disclosure lives in the caption and the platform's AI label, never burned in (agency rule, 5 November 2026).
