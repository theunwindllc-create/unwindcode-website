TRANSMISSION 026

The Property Sales
Intelligence Cell

Real Estate Property Sales Intelligence is a governed cell for property teams that need faster signal without giving automation authority over people. This revision says what the record actually holds, what is only a design, and what has to be true before anything reaches a person: proof before outreach, and human approval before outreach.

June 2026, revised November 2026·Domain cell·Design record·9 min read
Dated. The first version was written on 6 June 2026. This revision describes the Brain repository, the design documents and the website’s claims registry as read on 10 November 2026. It describes a design. It does not describe a service that is running.
Revised 10 November 2026. The original named three consumer property platforms and two industry bodies as its "ecosystem signal". Those sources cannot be re-verified here and are removed. The five-bar authority meter, whose numbers were illustrative, is replaced by an ordered ladder with no numbers. The note now has a dated history of the cell, a status for every piece, and a gate panel that says which parts are design.
Correction, 10 November 2026. The June text said the Brain "looked at" the "live real estate CRM work" and that lead intake, import review, qualification, routing and follow-up planning were "active enough to deserve a public organism boundary". This revision found no record of a running real estate service. The record shows documents and designs, a cell whose source directory was retired on 13 August as an empty shell, and a restore on a review branch on 5 September that is not in the repository’s main line today. "Active" was too strong and is withdrawn.
01

A history longer than the note

Dated, from the repository and the calendar

The June note read as though a cell had just arrived. The record is older and more uneven. The earliest trace in the Brain repository is a commit dated 15 January 2026 that deposits a real estate knowledge package: standard operating procedures, an architecture document, lead-generation and follow-up scripts, buyer and seller guides, Florida contract notes and a domain research table. That is a body of knowledge for a cell, not a service.

On 13 August a reconciliation pass found that the cell’s source directory survived only as an empty shell, while the identity documents advertised it as live. The directory was removed and the retirement was recorded rather than erased. On 5 September, on the operator’s spoken word, a restore was prepared on a review branch. The restore is recorded in the journal; that branch is not in the repository’s local branch list on 10 November.

The timeline below follows the track. Read the last two steps carefully: the wiring of the cell into the organism is still a spec that waits on a reviewer and on the operator.

02

What exists, what is design, what waits

A status for every piece

The panel sorts the pieces by what can be checked on 10 November. "Exists" is a document, a script or a registry entry you can open. "Design" is a structure the documents describe and nothing runs. "Waits" is a step that needs the operator’s hand: a key, a host, a spending cap, a read of live data. "Removed" is something the original said and this revision no longer does.

The sorting is the point. The June note spoke in one tense for all of it.

03

Four gates, as a model

Design · nothing here is running

The design’s safety idea is that preparing motion and owning motion are different. A record can be read, scored and turned into a draft. Whether any of it leaves is a different question that a human answers at the end of a chain of checks. The original named the chain: source, consent, policy, human.

The panel runs that chain. The left column is a lead blaster: a list arrives and messages go out. The right column is the design, in order. Select a scenario to see where it stops. The most important line is the same in every scenario, including the clean one. In the spec the organ has no send path at all. The best case ends at a draft that waits for a person. The first gate asks whether a record came from one of the permitted lead sources, with its evidence kept.

      The authority ladder
