## Visual interpretation

Infinity represents continuity through feedback, not unbounded autonomy. This is an editorial interpretation of the architecture, not an official runtime diagram. Two lobes connect acting and reflecting, with the human boundary visible at the crossing. Every complete loop returns through proof and memory.

The flagship is a glass infinity sculpture, carrying the approved violet mineral/filament material family. The interactive companion is a genuine parametric three-dimensional curve rendered with native WebGL, not a prerecorded animation or live telemetry. Six manually selected explanations make the loop inspectable: sense, remember, reason, authorize, act, learn. The anatomy retains the actual six named organs; loop stages are not a replacement organ taxonomy.

Product organisms are visual variations of one material vocabulary. Memory rings explain distinct forms of recall. The philosophy section uses the current doctrine rather than generic innovation slogans. Proof links and transmission records remain primary evidence; generated art is expressly conceptual.
