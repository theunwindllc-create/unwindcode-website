Non-negotiables

The principles are product constraints.

These are not slogans on a wall. They decide how Unwind Code builds AI agents, Web3 simulations, cognitive organisms, and public proof.

I

Truth over convenience

Every public claim needs evidence: architecture, tests, transmissions, status labels, or an explicit unknown.

II

Human authority remains intact

The organism can recommend, simulate, draft, and warn. It cannot silently seize the operator's authority.

III

Everything important is replaceable

Cells, providers, memory stores, gateways, and tools must be swappable without destroying the identity of the system.

IV

Clarity before motion

Speed is not wisdom. The system acts only after it can explain what it sees, what it risks, and who approves.

V

Preserve the light

The point of intelligence is not domination. It is clearer attention, safer agency, and more room for people to become whole.
