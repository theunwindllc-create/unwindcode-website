Transmission 027 names the Quotation Cell.

A quote is not a form submission. It is a governed transaction packet: customer intent, line items, supplier options, pricing evidence, recommendation state, correction history, and human approval before commitment.

Until the Brain can run this loop directly, this transmission records the manual Brain protocol: real signal, organism pattern, build recipe, approval boundary, proof metadata, and deployment verification.

The cell can capture intake, compare options, preserve corrections, draft a package, mark readiness, and route review. It does not promise price, availability, purchasing, fulfillment, legal status, or commercial outcome.

This is the Unwind pattern moving into another field: the frontend becomes the command surface, the backend becomes the domain organ, and the proof trail decides when work is ready to leave the organism.
