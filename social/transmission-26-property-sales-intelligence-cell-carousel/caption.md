# Instagram Carousel Caption

Real estate AI should not begin with blasting leads.

It should begin with proof:

- Where did this lead come from?
- What consent exists?
- What intent is actually known?
- What must be asked next?
- What should be blocked or escalated?

The Property Sales Intelligence Cell is the UnwindCode frame for a real estate CRM that treats sales speed as a governed system.

Buyer, seller, renter, and investor signals move through source review, qualification, compliance gates, human approval, and audit logs before any action leaves the system.

The point is not to replace the agent.

The point is to help the agent see who is ready, who needs nurturing, who asked to stop, and who needs human judgment now.
