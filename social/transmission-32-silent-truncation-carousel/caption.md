# Transmission 32: The Silent Truncation

The most dangerous failures don't crash.

They are absorbed as valid input.

A merge cut two closing braces out of our stylesheet. Under native CSS nesting, the wound was legal syntax — the final 1,300 lines of presentation law were silently re-parented under an image element that never contained them.

Every mobile rule. Every lab-page style. Dead for eleven days.

Builds green. Tests green. No error anyone read.

The same week, a second silent wound: 61 of 62 sitemap URLs redirected away from the pages they named. The organism's public name disagreed with its served form — and search infrastructure recorded the disagreement as failure.

One failure class, two wounds: valid-but-wrong.

- CSS nesting made a missing brace a feature
- clean-URL routing made a wrong link a redirect
- permissive registries made a stale digest just data

Each choice is locally reasonable. Together they cost the organism its ability to feel pain in exactly the layers it shows the world.

The healing law — three invariants, now enforced:

I. Structural integrity — the artifact parses to what was written.
II. Name/form parity — every published URL resolves to the surface it names.
III. Provenance continuity — names and hashes stay bound under change.

Healed and live-verified: 62/62 URLs answer 200. Zero poisoned selectors. Zero broken phone layouts.

The skin is an organ. Organs get immune coverage.
