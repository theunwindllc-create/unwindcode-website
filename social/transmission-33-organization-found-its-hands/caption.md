# Transmission 33: The Organization Found Its Hands

Fourteen departments existed on paper. Zero ran as agents.

That is prompt-theater: organizational language without separate runtime minds.

The repair was not to make every department autonomous. It was to classify the organism correctly:

- personas carry durable identity and bounded authority
- children run bounded work in separate sessions and return receipts
- tool playbooks are hands, not minds
- the operator routes and collects
- the human retains identity and publication authority

The first real child session wrote an artifact and returned a receipt. It also revealed the missing orchestration law: the parent ended 16 seconds before the child finished.

Spawn is not collection.

WAIT LAW: do not leave before your children come home.

The organization becomes real when identity, work, evidence, collection, and authority remain in one visible chain.
