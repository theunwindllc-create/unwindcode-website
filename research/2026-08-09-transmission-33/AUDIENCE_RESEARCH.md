# Audience Research — Transmission 33

## Primary Audience — Agent-System Builders

Goals: distinguish roles, tools, and agents; design bounded delegation; control costs; prevent orphaned child work.

Questions:

- What makes an agent a separate mind rather than a prompt section?
- What must a parent wait for?
- Who may amend identity?
- Where does human approval remain mandatory?

Needed proof: runtime receipts, depth policy, artifact names, failure logs, amendment semantics.

## Secondary Audience — Operators and Technical Founders

Goals: understand whether an “agent organization” is governable and economical.

Objections:

- More agents may multiply cost and confusion.
- Persona identity may become brand risk.
- Self-amending charters may become hidden authority.

Answer: expensive models orchestrate and judge; cheaper children execute; agents propose amendments but cannot edit charters; identity-level changes and publication remain human-gated.

## Tertiary Audience — Public Readers Following Unwind

Goals: understand what changed without private infrastructure detail.

Needed language: “hands, not minds,” “prompt-theater,” WAIT LAW, and a visual progression from charter to receipt.

## Journey
