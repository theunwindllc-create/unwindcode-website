## Current Brand Character

Architectural, sharp, minimal, evidence-led, organism-native. The strongest existing pages use a dark void, monospace operational labels, restrained color, whitepaper sections, and inspectable interactions (`style.css`, `transmissions/32-the-silent-truncation.html`).

## Positioning

Unwind Code should own the distinction between departments described in prose and agents proved in runtime. The transmission's core diagnosis is **prompt-theater**; its mechanism is identity-shaped execution under explicit authority.

## Strengths

- A precise organism vocabulary already exists.
- The fact pack contains dated, public-safe proof artifacts.
- The failure is instructive: the parent exited before the child completed.
- The privacy boundary can be demonstrated, not merely stated.

## Risks

- Overclaiming “organization” as unattended autonomy.
- Confusing playbooks with independent agents.
- Treating a successful spawn as proof of reliable orchestration.
- Leaking a pre-public identity through copy, metadata, JSON, tests, or research artifacts.
- Turning the paper into a celebratory launch note.

## Recommended Position

The organization found hands only when three conditions became inspectable: a persistent identity, a bounded runtime session, and a receipt returned to the parent. Hands without collection discipline are abandoned work; identity without proof is theater.
