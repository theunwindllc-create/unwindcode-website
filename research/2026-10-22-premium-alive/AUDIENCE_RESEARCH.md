# Audience research — 2026-10-22

## Evidence

The homepage offers organisms, system explanation, philosophy, transmissions, public proof and a build-with-us route. It supports English and Spanish. Evidence: `public/lab/index.html` and its existing destination routes.

## Working audience hypotheses

| Audience inference | Likely question | Experience requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prospective collaborator | What do you build, and can I trust the work? | Immediate promise, concrete organism examples, reachable proof and contact route. |
| Technical evaluator | How does the architecture work and where are its limits? | Inspectable loop, qualified claims and readable deep links. |
| Curious first-time visitor | What am I looking at? | A short visual invitation with clear language, useful responses to scroll or selection. |
| Returning reader | What is new? | Direct transmissions navigation and no repeated entrance obstruction. |

These are hypotheses derived from the site's structure, not interview or analytics findings. No demographic, traffic, conversion or buying-intent claims are established.

## Inclusive use cases

Test mouse, touch, keyboard, narrow screens, language switching, reduced motion, motion pause and no JavaScript. Scrolling must remain native. Moving artwork must never displace essential copy, obscure controls or become the only way to learn a fact.

## Revision 2: mobile first

Treat mobile network and decoded-image memory as first-class constraints. A visitor can arrive with a cold cache, limited bandwidth, portrait or landscape orientation, browser UI changing the viewport height, and no hover. The initial static composition must be complete before any cinematic enhancement arrives. Scroll direction controls the sequence; missing frames retain the nearest decoded frame without a loader, blank canvas or blocked input. Reduced-motion and data-saving visitors receive the composed still with full content.
