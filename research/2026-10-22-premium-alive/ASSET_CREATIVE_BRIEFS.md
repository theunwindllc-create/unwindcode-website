## Treatment brief

Preserve original shape, color relationships and conceptual status. For the hero, use image-to-video to animate the same sculpture: shallow camera movement, stable topology, one quiet filament trace, near-black backdrop and consistent empty space. Do not invent unseen biological anatomy or show a portal journey. Keep the sculpture recognizable at every frame and its brightest crossing clear of text. Maintain a horizontal composition usable within the existing mobile hero rather than cropping it into an unrelated vertical shot.

Deliver one 4–6 second master without audio, plus a contact sheet for start/quarter/mid/three-quarter/end and detailed review of any suspect interpolation. Reject silhouette changes, extra lobes, melt, flashes, camera jumps, floating labels or newly invented symbols. From an accepted master create a maximum 24-frame 640×426 mobile rendition within 1 MiB and 36-frame 960×640 desktop rendition within 2 MiB. These are output budgets; lower quality is not automatically acceptable to meet them. If both visual integrity and budget cannot be met, keep the existing still/depth fallback and report the exact shortfall.

Record the actual generator, reference role, submitted settings, job ID, returned source path/hash, derived-frame hashes and bytes, and review limitations. The existing reference hashes above identify input files only, not a generated output or approval.
