# Audience Research

## Primary Audience

Builders, operators, and technical founders evaluating whether UnwindCode is building real AI systems or just conceptual content.

## Secondary Audience

Investors, partners, and collaborators who need to understand the safety posture without reading source code.

## Reader Needs

- What happened locally?
- What is public-safe to say?
- What does this unlock?
- What boundaries remain closed?

## Objections

- "Is this actually built?"
- "Is memory exposing private data?"
- "Does search generate answers without review?"
- "Does the system act automatically?"

## Recommended Trust Content

- Show source-backed routes and files.
- Keep claims bounded to local unpushed work.
- Repeat that public posting, deployment, and mutation remain approval-gated.
